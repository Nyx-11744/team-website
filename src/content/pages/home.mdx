## Team Nyx is a *FIRST* Robotics Competition team (#11744) founded in San Diego, California.

FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) is a global high-school robotics program where teams design, build, program, and compete with industrial-scale robots in a new game each season. Beyond the robot, FRC emphasizes engineering, teamwork, leadership, and outreach through a real-world, collaborative competition environment.
